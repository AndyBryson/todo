Todo: privacy

Todo is a personal front end for Google Tasks. It is a static web app with no server of its own.

What it accesses

  • Google Tasks: your task lists and tasks, to show and edit them.
  • Google Drive app data folder: one hidden file, board.json, that holds each task's board column, order and pin. It holds task IDs only, never task titles or notes. Todo cannot see any other Drive files.

Where your data goes

Your data stays in your Google account and in your browser. Todo keeps a copy in your browser's local storage so it works offline. It talks only to Google's own services and sends nothing to any other server. There is no tracking or analytics.

Removing access

You can revoke Todo's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the local copy.
